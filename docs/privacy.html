Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

TaskCanvas ("the app") is a macOS app that connects to your Google Tasks account to display and manage your tasks. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data We Access

TaskCanvas requests access to your Google Tasks data (task lists and tasks) via the Google Tasks API. This access is used solely to read and update your tasks within the app.

Data Storage

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain with the kSecAttrAccessibleWhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly protection class. Tokens are not stored in the task cache or sent to our servers.
  • A snapshot of your task data is cached locally in the app's sandboxed App Group container so the app and widget can display it.
  • All communication with Google uses HTTPS/TLS. The app uses OAuth 2.0 with PKCE and validates the OAuth state before accepting a callback.
  • TaskCanvas has no backend database and does not transmit Google user data to our servers.

Data Protection

We protect sensitive data using the macOS sandbox, access-controlled App Group storage, and the macOS Keychain. OAuth tokens are restricted to the unlocked device on which they were created. Network requests use HTTPS/TLS, and OAuth authorization uses PKCE and state validation to reduce interception and request-forgery risks. We do not log task contents, OAuth tokens, or refresh tokens.

Data Sharing

TaskCanvas does not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third parties. All communication is directly between your device and Google's APIs.

Google API Scopes

The app requests the following Google API scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks — Read and write access to your Google Tasks.

Limited Use Disclosure

TaskCanvas's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. TaskCanvas only uses Google Tasks data to provide and improve the app's task-viewing and task-management features for the signed-in user, and does not transfer or sell this data to third parties.

Revoking Access

You can revoke TaskCanvas's access to your Google account at any time via Google Account Permissions. You can also sign out from within the app's Settings.

Data Retention and Deletion

TaskCanvas retains Google Tasks data only as a local cache while you use the app, so the app and widget can work between refreshes. We do not retain Google user data on our servers. When you sign out, TaskCanvas deletes the local task snapshots and OAuth tokens from the app's storage and Keychain. Uninstalling the app removes its sandbox and App Group files according to macOS behavior. Tasks that you create or keep in Google Tasks remain in your Google account until you delete them there; TaskCanvas does not delete Google account data merely because you sign out.

To request help confirming deletion of any locally stored data, contact ru.japan.info@gmail.com. To delete data held by Google, use Google Tasks or your Google Account controls.
